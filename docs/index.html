What it does

Scan, remove, monitor. On its own.

Most of data-broker removal is repetitive work: search, match, send the right request, wait, check again. That part is automated, and you can see every step.

Scan

Every name, nickname, former city and phone you add is searched on 430+ sites. Matches are scored 0–100 on name, age, city and relatives, with a screenshot kept as evidence.

  • Weekly, and after every profile change
  • Site coverage shows which sites answer

Remove

The best channel is chosen for each broker: parent-company suppression, the web form or a privacy email. Each request is written for the person's state and sent as their authorized agent.

  • Only lists what the broker showed
  • Verification links clicked for you

Monitor

Reminder on day 20, escalation on day 45. A listing is marked Removed only after a re-scan confirms it's gone, then re-checked weekly and re-filed if it comes back.

  • Weekly email per family member
  • Heartbeat shows the health of every job

Needs you

A short list, not a second job.

The few steps that need a person are on one page, grouped by person, each with the reason in plain language and one button to do it.

  • Blocked sites retry first. They're retried from your server's own IP after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 days before they reach you.
  • CAPTCHA solving is optional. 2Captcha or CapSolver with a hard monthly budget. Off by default, with a cost estimate shown.
  • Human-only steps are labeled. California DROP residency, the person's own signature, photo-ID uploads and phone-only opt-outs.

How it works

One lifecycle for every listing.

Every step runs on a schedule inside the container. Dry run is on until you turn it off, so you can read every request before anything is sent.

  1. 01Scan

    Headless Chromium, one site at a time, with a polite delay.

  2. 02Match

    Scored 0–100. Low scores are shown but never acted on.

  3. 03Approve

    By the person, the admin, or automatically above a score you set.

  4. 04Submit

    Parent suppression, form or email, whichever has worked for that broker.

  5. 05Verify

    Broker confirmation emails arrive and their links are opened.

  6. 06Follow up

    Reminder on day 20, escalation that cites the regulator on day 45.

  7. 07Confirm

    A re-scan shows the page is gone. Marked Removed.

  8. 08Watch

    Re-checked weekly. Re-filed automatically if it comes back.

Self-host in minutes

Three commands. Then a setup wizard.

You don't edit config files. Email keys, schedules, branding and safety switches all live in the app under Heartbeat → Settings, and take effect without a restart.

Home serverDocker or Portainer, Cloudflare Tunnel. More sites answer from home IPs. $5 VPSHetzner, DigitalOcean, Vultr. HTTPS through the bundled Caddy. Fly.ioOne machine, a 1 GB volume, one secret. RailwayDeploy from the repo and add a volume.
bash
# 1. get the code
$ git clone https://github.com/fletcher-fortis/fletcher-fortis.git && cd fletcher-fortis
# 2. start it (dry run is on)
$ docker compose up -d
# 3. open the setup wizard
$ open http://localhost:8080
Needs about 1 GB RAM. Email through Resend (free tier) and Cloudflare Email Routing (free).

Privacy and security

Built to hold the data it removes.

A removal tool knows exactly what you're trying to hide, so it's designed to leak as little as possible if anything goes wrong.

Encrypted at rest

Every personal field is encrypted with AES-256-SIV under a key only you hold. A copy of the volume alone is unreadable.

No identity documents

The app refuses Social Security numbers, full dates of birth and ID numbers. The schema has nowhere to put them.

Locked-down container

Non-root user, read-only filesystem, all Linux capabilities dropped. Only the data volume is writable.

Hardened web app

Server-side sessions, bcrypt passwords, login rate limits, CSRF tokens and a strict Content-Security-Policy.

Family-scoped access

Members see only their own profile and listings. Only the admin can turn sending on.

No telemetry

The app talks only to broker sites and your email provider. Nothing else phones home.

Requests are sent as an authorized agent, only for people who signed the authorization themselves. Use it for yourself and family members who agree. Not legal advice.
